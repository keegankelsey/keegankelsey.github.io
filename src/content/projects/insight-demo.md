Fellows at Insight Data Science are expected to rapidly build a unique and novel data project for presentation to companies. The goal is to demonstrate the ability to develop, from scratch, product insight from data. The specific project I worked on was fun and challenging and involved mining masked text-messaging data for a type of sentiment. Although the code is very clearly not satisfactory for production, it sufficed in generating a proof of concept and clearly demonstrated strong structure in the data.
