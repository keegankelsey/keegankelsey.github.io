Keegan Kelsey, Ph.D., is a scientist, an engineer, and a creative. Most recently a Director of Engineering at [DNAnexus](https://www.dnanexus.com/), Dr. Kelsey collaborates extensively across an organization (Product, Engineering, Scientific, Business Units, Marketing, and Executive teams), supporting the progress and development of data products. With a specialization in clinical and multi-omic data, he has over 10 years of experience in the technology/biotechnology sectors and over 18 years of experience in scientific research.

Dr. Kelsey received his doctorate from Cornell University in Ithaca, New York, in 2016. He was an Insight Data Science Fellow in 2015 and was subsequently a Data Scientist at 23andMe. He received his bachelor's degree from The School of the Art Institute of Chicago.

Past research and engineering efforts have involved a diverse array of topics in both human and model organisms, with direct bioinformatics, software engineering, and analysis experience in genetics, population genetics (germline and somatic), clinical/phenotypic data, -omic data (genomic, transcriptomic, epigenomic, proteomic), and metadata such as annotations and ontologies. Currently, efforts are spent exploring, optimizing, integrating, inferring and analyzing disparate data with an emphasis on data-centric discovery and product delivery.
